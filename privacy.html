适用范围与联系方式

本政策适用于久物（Jiuwu）iOS、Android 应用及与其绑定的微信小程序账户数据。邮箱注册或登录后可独立使用 iOS 和 Android 应用；iOS 还可选择“通过 Apple 登录”。微信绑定为可选功能，主动绑定后保留两端记录并合并账户数据，用于跨端同步。iOS 和 Android 包名均为 com.jiuwu.longtermism。开发者：张益铭。隐私及数据请求联系邮箱：zhangyiming001124@163.com。

我们处理哪些信息、为什么处理

  • 账户信息：邮箱地址、认证标识及会话信息，用于注册、登录、账号安全、更换邮箱。用户主动绑定微信时，处理微信账户标识及绑定关系，用于跨端同步。
  • 用户主动录入的数据：物品、金额、账目、备注、使用及打卡记录、生活卡、份子钱等，用于保存记录、统计展示及云端同步。反馈功能处理用户提交的反馈内容。
  • 会员与购买信息：Google Play 商品、购买令牌、订单标识、订阅状态及关联账户，用于验证购买、恢复权益及核对续订状态。支付由 Google Play 处理；应用不接收完整银行卡号或银行卡安全码。
  • iOS 账户与购买信息：当您选择“通过 Apple 登录”时，我们处理 Apple 提供的授权标识和您选择共享的邮箱地址，用于创建或登录久物账户；若您使用“隐藏邮箱”，我们仅收到 Apple 转发地址。iOS 端会员为一次性购买的非消耗型项目；我们处理 Apple 交易标识、商品标识、购买状态和关联账户，用于验证购买、恢复权益及防止重复发放。支付由 Apple 处理；应用不接收完整银行卡号或银行卡安全码。
  • 照片：仅在您主动选择照片或拍摄照片时，应用处理所选照片，用作物品图片并上传到您的云端记录。应用不读取您的整个照片图库；已上传照片仅在验证账户权限后提供访问。
  • 地区设置：经系统许可后，应用可读取大致位置并通过 Android 地理编码服务识别国家，用于设置默认语言和货币。该功能不进行持续位置追踪，应用业务接口不上传经纬度。您可拒绝或撤销权限，并手动选择语言和货币；地理编码服务可能由设备的服务提供方处理位置查询。
  • 设备、运行及运营信息：为维持登录安全，应用会生成随机设备标识，并处理访问时间、账户标识，以及服务运行产生的请求和错误日志，用于认证、安全维护和故障排查。网络服务提供方可能处理 IP 地址等连接信息。

服务提供方与数据存储

账户认证及云端业务数据使用腾讯云 CloudBase，当前业务环境位于中国上海。您在境外使用应用时，向云端提交的数据会传输至该环境。微信处理微信登录和小程序服务，Google Play 处理 Android 订阅购买及交易验证；Apple 处理 iOS 的 Apple 登录和 App Store 支付。Android 系统及其地理编码服务提供方处理相关系统功能。我们按上述功能所需向相应服务提供方传递信息。

应用使用 HTTPS 与云端及购买验证服务通信，使用账户认证和访问控制限制数据访问。设备上会保存会话、设置及相关缓存；若您主动选择保留密码，该密码只保存在设备的系统安全存储中。腾讯云、微信、Google 和 Apple 另有各自的隐私政策：腾讯云、微信、Google、Apple。

数据保留与用户控制

云端记录在您使用服务期间保留，直到您删除记录或请求删除相应数据。更换绑定邮箱会保留同一业务账户的数据及权益。当前“移除 App 登录账号 / Remove App login”或“注销邮箱账号 / Delete email account”仅删除相应登录身份，保留业务数据；这与完整账户及数据删除不同。退出登录不删除云端数据。您可使用应用的记录删除、清空物品和账目功能，或通过下方邮箱提出更完整的删除请求。

必要的交易、安全及依法必须保留的信息可能在满足相应义务所需期间保留；备份或服务日志中的副本可能无法立即清除。删除账号或数据不会自动取消 Google Play 订阅，请在 Google Play 的订阅管理中取消续订。

儿童与政策更新

本应用并非面向儿童设计。若您发现儿童未经适当授权提交了个人信息，请联系我们处理。功能或数据处理方式变化时，我们会更新本页面及生效日期；需要另行取得许可的处理会按适用要求取得许可。本应用不出售个人信息，也不将上述信息用于跨 App 或跨网站跟踪广告。

Jiuwu Privacy Policy

Scope and contact

This policy covers the Jiuwu iOS and Android apps (both com.jiuwu.longtermism) and account data linked with its WeChat mini program. You can use the iOS and Android apps independently with an email account; iOS also offers Sign in with Apple. WeChat linking is optional; when you choose to link, records from both platforms are retained and account data is merged for cross-platform sync. Developer: Zhang Yiming (张益铭). Contact for privacy and data requests: zhangyiming001124@163.com.

Information processed and purposes

  • Account information: email address, authentication identifiers and sessions for registration, sign-in, security and email changes. When you link WeChat, WeChat identifiers and linking records support synchronization between platforms.
  • Information you enter: items, amounts, ledger entries, notes, usage and check-in records, stored cards and gift-money records for storage, summaries and cloud synchronization. Feedback includes the content you submit.
  • Purchases: Google Play products, purchase tokens, order identifiers, subscription status and account associations for verification, entitlement restoration and renewal checks. Google Play handles payments; the app does not receive full payment card numbers or card security codes.
  • iOS accounts and purchases: when you choose Sign in with Apple, we process the authorization identifier and email address that Apple provides or that you choose to share, to create or sign in to a Jiuwu account. If you use Hide My Email, we receive only Apple's relay address. The iOS membership is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. We process Apple transaction identifiers, product identifiers, purchase status and the associated account to verify purchases, restore entitlements and prevent duplicate grants. Apple handles payment; the app does not receive full payment card numbers or card security codes.
  • Photos: only when you choose a photo or take one, the app processes that selected photo as an item image and uploads it to your cloud record. The app does not read your entire photo library. Uploaded photos are made available only after account authorization.
  • Regional preferences: with system permission, the app can read approximate location and use Android geocoding to identify a country for default language and currency. It does not continuously track location or upload coordinates through its business APIs. You can decline or revoke permission and choose settings manually. The device's geocoding service provider may process location queries.
  • Device and operations: to keep sign-in secure, the app generates a random device identifier and processes visit times, account identifiers, and service request/error logs for authentication, security and troubleshooting. Network providers may process IP addresses and connection information.

Providers, storage and security

Tencent CloudBase provides authentication and cloud storage. The current business environment is in Shanghai, China; data submitted from other countries is transmitted to this environment. WeChat provides WeChat and mini program services. Google Play processes Android subscriptions and transaction verification. Apple provides Apple sign-in and iOS App Store payment. Android and device geocoding providers supply system functions. Information is passed to these providers as needed for the functions described above.

The app communicates with cloud and verification services using HTTPS and uses authentication and access controls. Sessions, preferences and relevant caches are stored on the device. If you opt to retain a password, it stays only in the device's system-secure storage. Provider policies: Tencent Cloud, WeChat, Google, and Apple.

Retention and controls

Cloud records are retained during your use of the service until you delete them or request deletion. Changing email preserves business data and entitlements. The current “Remove App login” or “Delete email account” feature deletes only the relevant sign-in identity and preserves business data; it is different from full account and data deletion. Signing out does not erase cloud data. You can delete records or clear items and ledger entries in the app, or contact us below for broader deletion requests.

Transaction, security or legally required information may be retained for the period needed to meet those obligations. Copies in backups or service logs may not disappear immediately. Account or data deletion does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription; cancel renewal in Google Play subscription management.

Children and changes

The app is not designed for children. Contact us if a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorization. We update this page and its effective date when functions or data practices change, and obtain additional permission where applicable. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-app or cross-site advertising tracking.
